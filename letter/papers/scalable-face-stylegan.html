摘要

Face images are a special case: their content is highly structured, and a generative prior already knows most of it.

人脸图像是一个特例：内容高度结构化，生成先验其实已经“知道”了其中大部分。

The paper asks whether a scalable codec can push most of the bits into the latent, leaving the generator to reconstruct the rest.

这篇论文问的是：可分级编码能不能把大部分比特压进隐变量，剩下的交给生成器重建。
