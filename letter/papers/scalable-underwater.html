02

摘要

Underwater images degrade in a way that differs from natural images: colour casts, scattering and low contrast dominate the statistics.

水下图像的退化方式与自然图像不同：偏色、散射和低对比度主导了它的统计特性。

A scalable codec for this setting must decide how much of the budget goes to what a diver can see and what a detector needs.

在这种场景下，可分级编码要决定：预算里有多少给人眼看到的细节，多少给检测器需要的线索。
